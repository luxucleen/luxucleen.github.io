Engine assumptions (read before trusting any number):
  • Daily bars only. Your onBar runs once per bar, after the close.
  • One position at a time. buy/sell while a position is open are ignored.
  • Fixed 0.01 lot, $1,000 starting balance, no compounding.
  • Orders fill at the bar’s close. Stops/targets are checked from the next bar; if a bar hits both stop and target, the stop is assumed hit first (conservative).
  • Spread cost per round trip: XAUUSD 35 points, EURUSD 20 points, GBPUSD 25 points. Uploaded symbols: 20 points.
  • No slippage modeling. Past performance says nothing about the future.
Lab API:
  • onBar(bar, i, ctx) — bar = {date, open, high, low, close}, i = bar index.
  • ctx.buy(price, sl, tp) / ctx.sell(price, sl, tp) — price optional (defaults to close).
  • ctx.closeAll() — exits at this bar’s close. ctx.position — null or {type, entry, sl, tp}.
  • Indicators (offset 0 = this bar, 1 = last bar): ctx.ema(p, off), ctx.sma(p, off), ctx.rsi(p, off), ctx.atr(p, off), ctx.macd(f, s, sig, off) → {macd, signal, hist}, ctx.stoch(k, d, off) → {k, d}, ctx.highestHigh(p, off), ctx.lowestLow(p, off).
