GRID28 — an expert advisor for MetaTrader 4

GRID28 trades gold on the hourly chart. It only opens in the direction of the slow trend, the 100-bar average. Each time price moves one more step in its favour, it adds the same fixed lot, never a bigger one, up to ten steps. It holds the whole basket behind a lock that sits three times the average hourly range behind the best price, and it closes the basket when its profit target is reached. It is written for MetaTrader 4 (build 600 or newer).

  • The lock is not a stop order at your broker. GRID28 checks it when each hourly bar closes, so a fast move inside the hour can go past it, and nothing protects the trades while MetaTrader is closed.
  • It is a trend-rider. It wins rarely and wins big, rather than often and small. Trade a size you can afford to lose.
  • It runs without a licence on a demo account. A live account needs a licence key tied to your MT4 account number.
