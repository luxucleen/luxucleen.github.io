How it works

  1. We land the contract. Luxucleen LLC bids and signs the client — apartments, HOAs, commercial. Big clients want a licensed LLC with insurance, and that is exactly what we are.
  2. A vetted crew does the work. A landscaper with their own equipment, working under our agreement, is matched to the job close to them.
  3. The client pays Luxucleen, and we pay the crew. Fast, on schedule, better than waiting weeks — you keep the lion's share of the job, we keep a small cut for landing and managing it.

Why the LLC matters. Most apartment complexes, HOAs and businesses will only hire a company with an LLC, a certificate of insurance and a real contract — not an individual with a mower. Luxucleen LLC is that company, so a good crew can finally reach the bigger, steady, better-paying contracts they could not land on their own.

Honest terms

  • Crews are independent contractors who bring their own equipment; a simple written agreement sets the pay and the schedule before any work starts. This is not an offer of employment.
