How it works

  1. Tell us what to watchOne message - what the deadline is for, the date (or how often), and how to reach you. Takes a minute.
  2. We remind you before it is dueA real voice call, a text, or an email - whichever you picked - ahead of the date, so there is time to act.
  3. You act in timeRenew the benefit, file the claim, show up for the date. We remind; you decide. Reply STOP any time to end it.

What we can watch for you

  • Benefit renewals. Medicaid, SNAP, or other benefits that quietly lapse if you miss the recertification date.
  • Money the county owes you. A tax-deed surplus claim window has a hard deadline - miss it and the money can be lost.
  • Court & small-claims dates. A missed date can mean a default judgment. We remind you it is coming.
  • Licenses & registrations. A driver's license, a business registration, a car tag - renew before the late fee.
  • Estate & probate deadlines. Filing windows after a death are short and easy to miss while grieving.
  • Anything with a date. A bill, an appointment, a document that expires - you name it, we watch it.

Your safety comes first

  • We never ask for secrets. No passwords, no Social Security number, no card numbers, no ID documents - ever. A reminder does not need any of that.
  • Opt-in only. We remind you about the things you asked us to watch, and nothing else. Reply STOP or email us and it ends immediately.
  • A reminder, not advice. We tell you a date is coming. We are not your lawyer or your accountant - for the decision itself, consult a professional.
