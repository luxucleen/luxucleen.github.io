Luxucleen LLC › Affiliates

Sell what we make and keep a share of every sale.

Luxucleen runs its affiliate program through Gumroad's own system. You get your own link, Gumroad tracks the sale, and your share is paid out of it automatically. It costs you nothing to join and costs us nothing unless you sell something.

How it works

  1. Ask for the products you want to promote. Email affiliates@luxucleen.com with the email on your Gumroad account and which products you want. Approval is by hand, by a person, so tell us where you would be promoting them.
  2. You get your own link back. One link per product, tied to your Gumroad account. Anything bought through it is credited to you.
  3. Gumroad pays you out of each sale. The commission comes off the sale automatically, so there is nothing to invoice and nothing to chase. On a monthly plan, Gumroad locks your percentage when you sign up and pays it on every renewal for the life of that subscription.

What we pay

These are the rates we work to. They are set per product inside Gumroad when your link is created.

35% — one-time, higher priced

GRID28 lifetime licence, and the one-page business website.

30% — one-time products

The Phone Test.

20% — monthly plans, on every renewal

GRID28 monthly, the B28.Signals rooms, PROOF28 and EYES28 monthly. A lower rate that keeps paying for as long as the subscriber stays.

Pay-what-you-want items are not part of the program, because nobody can be told in advance what a sale would pay.

What you would be promoting

The full list, with live prices, is on the store page. The two easiest things to talk about are the free phone check, which costs a business nothing to try, and GRID28, which runs free on a demo account for as long as someone wants to test it.

The rules, in plain words

  • No promises about money. Do not tell anyone what they will earn, with our products or with trading. Trading involves risk and the trading products are 18+ only.
  • No invented results. Do not write reviews, testimonials or numbers that did not happen. If you want a measured figure to quote, ask us for one.
  • No spam. No unsolicited bulk email or texts, no paid search on our brand names, and nothing that would embarrass a customer who sees it.
  • Say you are an affiliate. Where you are required to disclose a paid link, disclose it.
  • We can end an affiliate arrangement at any time if any of the above is broken. Sales already made are still paid.

Apply

Send your Gumroad email and the product you want to promote to affiliates@luxucleen.com, and you get your own link back. A person reads every application, so it is not instant.
