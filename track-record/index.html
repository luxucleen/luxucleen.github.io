4. Why we refuse to promise

Six trades is a tiny sample, and we will say so right here for as long as it is true. These figures are the strategy’s own as recorded on a paper account; they have not been independently verified yet, which is exactly what step 2 is for. A paper account has none of the slippage a real one does, and a good run of six is not a forecast of the seventh. Everyone else in this business promises returns. We show receipts and keep the honest lines on the screen. That refusal is the brand.
