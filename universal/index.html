How it works

A library of everything — not a million dead pages

Every business and product in the world is a lot to keep as pages nobody reads. So the Universal Store doesn’t store pages — it generates the one you asked for, the moment you ask. Three ways to actually get it:

1 · Buy it

It’s listed, you check out, we handle sourcing and delivery. No card ever touches this site — checkout runs on a secure processor.

2 · Send an AI agent

Our AI voice agent calls the business for you — arranges the deal, gets the real price and the details, and reports back. You never sit on hold.

3 · The Magnet

We already found it cheaper somewhere else. We list it under the Amazon price and dropship it straight to you.

The Magnet

Lower than Amazon — or it isn’t here

The Magnet is a curated shelf of products we’ve already sourced below the Amazon price from another supplier, and dropship to you. The rule is simple and it’s the whole promise: if we can’t beat the Amazon price on it, it doesn’t go on the shelf. No filler, no products that only make Amazon money — just the ones worth having, for less.
