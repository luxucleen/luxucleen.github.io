Why clipping fails — and how Clipp fixes it

Everyone has the same editing tools now, so editing is not the edge anymore. The edge is content intelligence — knowing the exact moment that deserves to be a clip. That is the one thing generic auto-clippers guess at, and the one thing Clipp is built to do.

1. It reads for the hookClipp scans the whole transcript for the lines that open a loop, land a punch, or flip an expectation — the scroll-stoppers, not just the loud parts.
2. It tells you whyEach pick comes with the reason it would perform, so you learn your own patterns instead of guessing — your personal "clip brain."
3. It drafts the captionEvery moment comes with a punchy caption ready to tweak, so you go from a 40-minute video to postable clips in minutes.
The honest part. Clipp finds the moments and writes the captions — you cut them in whatever editor you like, or bring them to Luxucleen and we build the finished clips for you. And unlike the "scrape a hundred viral accounts" playbook, Clipp only ever reads your own content. We built Luxucleen to be unscrapable; we are not going to turn around and scrape everyone else.
