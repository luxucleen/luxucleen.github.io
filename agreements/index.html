Build-Your-Business Agreement (revenue share 12% / 88%)

Between Luxucleen LLC ("Luxucleen") and you ("Owner"), for the business idea you submitted.

  1. What Luxucleen does. After reviewing your idea and accepting it in writing, Luxucleen builds and runs it for you: the page or store, the way customers find it, the payment flow, and the day-to-day operation described in the attached plan. Not every idea can be built; acceptance is in writing.
  2. The split. Luxucleen keeps 12% of the business's net profits (revenue minus the direct costs listed in the plan); you keep 88%. Profits are calculated and paid monthly, with a plain statement showing revenue, costs and your share.
  3. What is yours. The idea, the business name and brand, the customer list and the profits are yours. Luxucleen's own tools, software and methods stay Luxucleen's.
  4. Costs. Direct costs needed to run the business (ads, domains, fees) are agreed in the plan before they are spent and are deducted before the split. No cost is charged to you without your written OK.
  5. Term and ending. Month to month. Either side can end with 30 days' written notice. When it ends, you keep the business and everything that is yours; Luxucleen's 12% stops on the end date, after the final statement is settled.
  6. No promises. Luxucleen does not promise profit, income, a timeline or a result. A business can make nothing. Nothing here is financial, legal or tax advice.
  7. Clean business only. Luxucleen will not build anything illegal, unsafe, or against its honesty rules, and may end the agreement immediately if the business turns out to be one of those.

PAPELES28 Navigation Service Agreement (immigration papers)

Between Luxucleen LLC and you ("Client").

  1. What Luxucleen does. A free, confidential first consult to see whether a legal path may exist; then, if you decide to go ahead: organizing and translating your documents, connecting you with a licensed immigration attorney in your state, and following the process with you.
  2. What Luxucleen is not. Luxucleen is NOT a law firm, NOT an attorney and NOT a notario, and gives NO legal advice. Only the licensed attorney gives legal advice and makes filings. Luxucleen never promises any immigration result.
  3. Fee. Luxucleen's navigation fee is $149, payable in parts. You pay it only if you decide to go ahead after the free consult. The attorney's own fee is separate, set by the attorney, and told to you in writing before anything starts. A tip is welcome but never required.
  4. Refund. If Luxucleen cannot connect you with a licensed attorney willing to review your case, the navigation fee is refunded in full.
  5. Confidentiality. Your information is shared only with the attorney reviewing your case, and never with the government or anyone else.
  6. Legal work only. Luxucleen does not help anyone work without legal authorization and will end the agreement if that is what is being asked.

DMV Errand Service Agreement (Virginia)

Between Luxucleen LLC and you ("Customer").

  1. What Luxucleen does. Runs the DMV errand you request — a registration, sticker, title, renewal or similar — online, by mail or in person, so you do not miss work. Some tasks (a photo, a road test, REAL ID) require you in person by law; Luxucleen tells you which before you pay.
  2. Fee. $49 for an errand Luxucleen can complete online or by mail; $99 when Luxucleen goes to the DMV as your agent. DMV's own fees are separate: you prepay them or reimburse them from the receipt.
  3. Your part. You provide the documents the DMV requires and, when the task needs it, sign the Virginia DMV power-of-attorney form (VSA-70) naming Luxucleen for that task only.
  4. Not a guarantee. The DMV decides. If the DMV declines the task for a reason outside Luxucleen's control (missing document, hold, eligibility), the service fee for work already done is not refunded, and Luxucleen tells you exactly what is needed to try again.
  5. Privacy. Your documents are used only for the task and are not kept afterward.

Florida Surplus Recovery Agreement

Between Luxucleen LLC and you ("Claimant"), for money a Florida county may hold from a tax-deed sale.

  1. What Luxucleen does. Locates the surplus, prepares the claim paperwork with a licensed attorney's review, and helps you file it with the county.
  2. Nothing up front. You pay nothing unless money is recovered.
  3. Fee. Luxucleen's fee is a percentage of the recovered amount, never more than 12%, the cap set by Florida Statutes section 45.033. The county pays you, the claimant; Luxucleen's fee is paid from the recovered funds as stated in writing before you sign.
  4. Your right to do it yourself. You may file this claim yourself, directly with the county clerk, at no cost. This agreement is a choice, not a requirement.
  5. No guarantee. The county decides whether and how much is paid.
