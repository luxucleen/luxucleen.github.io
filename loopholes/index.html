Not scams, not schemes — the real, legal moves rich families use every day, in plain words. Let a trust own your assets, get paid through a business, and claim the tax breaks most people never do. Each one is set up by a licensed professional, and Luxucleen connects you to the right one.

Own nothing, control everything

Let a trust or LLC own your assets

The rich rarely own their house or business in their own name — a trust or LLC owns it. That shields it from lawsuits, keeps it private, skips probate, and passes it to your kids cleanly. It is legal, common, and set up by an attorney.
