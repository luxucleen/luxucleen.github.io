How it works

  1. Pick a product. Any trending item — you buy no inventory and hold no stock.
  2. Bridge28 builds + publishes your store. A real store page at a live link (luxucleen’s AI — no code, no hosting to set up).
  3. Share it or run ads. When an order comes in, the supplier ships it straight to your customer and you keep the profit — and when it’s working, we build and run the whole store (you keep 88%).
  • Dropshipping has real costs — ad spend and the supplier’s price for each item. Your first store page is free; it is an AI-built test store to help you validate a product, not a promise of profit.
  • When money or a real store matters, a person answers: hello@luxucleen.com.
