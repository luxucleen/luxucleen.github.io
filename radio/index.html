How it works

1 · It's liveA real station streams 24/7. There's a short broadcast delay, so your change lands smoothly — no gap in the music.
2 · You shape itType what you want. The sound is cleaned and tuned in real time — hiss out, level even, the way you ask.
3 · One at a timeEveryone shares the dial. Your turn holds for a moment, then it passes to the next person in line. Fair, and it works.
A note: Luxucleen Radio is a fun, experimental live station. Music is streamed from a third-party source and shaped in real time; what you type changes the sound (bass, brightness, level, noise), not the songs. Be kind — it's a shared dial. This is a preview; features may change.
